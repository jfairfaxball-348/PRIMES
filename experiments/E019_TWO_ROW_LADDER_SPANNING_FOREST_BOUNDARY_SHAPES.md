## 1. One independent primitive and exact finite meaning

For integer n>=1 define the undirected simple **two-row ladder graph** L_n with vertices (i,t), (i,b) for 1<=i<=n. Its edges are all n vertical rungs {(i,t),(i,b)} and, for 1<=i<n, the two horizontal edges {(i,t),(i+1,t)} and {(i,b),(i+1,b)}. Every edge is unweighted and appears once. This finite graph, rather than any prime data, is the sole mathematical source.

A **boundary-anchored spanning forest** contains every vertex, has no cycles, and every connected component contains at least one of the two vertices in column n. Count it in two disjoint connectivity states: C_n = number of such forests in which the two final-column vertices are connected, and D_n = number in which they are disconnected. Equivalently, C_n counts spanning trees of L_n and D_n counts spanning forests with precisely two components each touching a different final vertex. At n=1 both counts equal 1 (rung selected, or empty edge set, respectively). There is no zero-column graph in the anchor grammar.

Freeze the exact boundary-extension transfer, in ordered state (C,D):
C_1=1, D_1=1;
C_(n+1)=3*C_n+D_n;
D_(n+1)=2*C_n+D_n.
Its matrix is M=[[3,1],[2,1]] and (C_n,D_n)^T=M^(n-1)*(1,1)^T. The local extension cases are completely fixed: from connected C, two horizontals without a new rung or either one horizontal with a rung lead to connected C (3 choices), while either one horizontal without a rung leads to disconnected D (2 choices); from disconnected D, both horizontals with a rung lead to C (1 choice), while both horizontals without a rung lead to D (1 choice). All other attachments abandon an old component or introduce a cycle. This specifies the graph count independently of prime labels, and supplies exact deterministic arithmetic semantics, not a claimed theorem about primes.

For **each** integer anchor x>=1, compute only c(x)=C_x mod x and d(x)=D_x mod x, each canonical in [0,x). The production method is exact binary exponentiation of M^(x-1) modulo x, followed by its action on [1,1], with O(log x) 2-by-2 modular products; no graph enumeration at high x, no float, no randomisation, no primality helper, no x-step loop. Bounded independent small-graph exhaustive enumeration and direct recurrence may be used later as synthetic validation references only, never as high-band exploration. The same definitions hold for prime and composite x.
