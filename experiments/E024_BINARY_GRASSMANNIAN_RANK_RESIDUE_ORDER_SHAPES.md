## 1. First-principles object, complete exact semantics and independence boundary

For each integer n>=3 and k in {2,3}, let `V_n=F_2^n` and let `G(n,k)` denote the number of distinct k-dimensional linear subspaces of `V_n`. Subspaces, not vectors, flags, bases, labelled-set blocks, ordered words, graphs, additive partitions, or integer divisors, are the underlying finite objects. Equivalently, count k x n full-rank binary matrices up to left multiplication by invertible k x k matrices. The unique reduced-row-echelon representative of every subspace gives an independent small finite enumerator, which is **reference semantics only**, not a viable high-n evaluator.

Freeze `D_2=3` and `D_3=21`. For k=2 or 3,
`G(n,k)= [product_{j=0}^{k-1}(2^(n-j)-1)] / [product_{j=0}^{k-1}(2^(k-j)-1)]`,
an **integer** by the full-rank-matrix/orbit-count definition. Define `m=x+1` for integer anchor `x>=3`. For each k, compute **only** the product of factors `(pow_mod(2,x-j,D_k*m)-1) mod (D_k*m)`, where modular exponentiation is ordinary deterministic binary repeated squaring and the products are reduced modulo `D_k*m` after every multiplication. Call the resulting canonical product `z_k` in `[0,D_k*m)`. Verify independently `z_k % D_k == 0`, then set `r_k=z_k//D_k` in `[0,m)`. This is exactly `G(x,k) mod (x+1)` because the integer numerator is divisible by `D_k` and reduction modulo `D_k*m` preserves the quotient modulo `m`. **Never** invert `D_k` modulo `x+1`; it need not be invertible. No high-size power, full matrix enumeration, floating arithmetic, factorization or primality test is part of the evaluator.

The **sole** eligible family F1 is a complete three-state comparison:
- `t(x)=0` iff `r_2<r_3`;
- `t(x)=1` iff `r_2=r_3`;
- `t(x)=2` iff `r_2>r_3`.

The ordered signature domain is exactly `[0,1,2]`; equality is a genuine separate state and must not be silently merged with either strict order. Raw `G`, `r_k`, `z_k`, quotient artifacts, multiplicative factors, individual subspace counts, ties forced by identities, subspace complements, comparisons with any other k/q or moduli, arithmetic-class predictions and all alternative encodings are **validation/triviality controls only** and never promotable.
