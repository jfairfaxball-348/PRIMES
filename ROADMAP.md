# PRIMES Roadmap

## D0 — Bootstrap

Build the authoritative records, exact prime core, tests, baseline experiment, and representation atlas.

**Exit:** reproducible E000 run and a frozen discovery protocol.

## D1 — Broad blind discovery

Generate many *observations*, not many conjectures.

Search across local geometry, cross-scale structure, residue dynamics, index/value duality, residuals, finite-object encodings, and symbolic-complexity diagnostics.

Use predeclared discovery and holdout ranges.

**Exit:** at most 20 replicated observations with exact definitions.

## D2 — Hostile compression

Attempt to destroy each observation.

Test parity, wheel/sieve effects, boundary effects, encoding artifacts, normalization artifacts, late-range failure, and simple known heuristics.

Merge equivalent observations.

**Exit:** at most 5 candidate-quality phenomena.

## D3 — Conjecture synthesis

Translate surviving phenomena into exact statements.

For each candidate:

- define the object;
- state the claim;
- identify weakest plausible form;
- identify strongest supported form;
- search aggressively for counterexamples;
- seek an explanatory mechanism.

**Exit:** at most 3 stressed candidates.

## D4 — Collision audit

Search exact and equivalent statements across the literature and databases.

The goal is not to "prove novelty" by search. The goal is to find collisions quickly and map the candidate into existing mathematical language.

Outcomes:

- known/equivalent;
- implied by known theorem;
- known in stronger form;
- adjacent but apparently distinct;
- no collision found within recorded scope.

**Exit:** at least one candidate that still merits expert review.

## D5 — Proof attack

Freeze the candidate statement and attack it mathematically.

Prefer reductions and structure over further brute-force confirmation.

If proof work becomes substantial, graduate the candidate to its own repository.

## D6 — Independent verification

Reproduce key computations independently, audit assumptions, and where appropriate formalise critical finite or deductive components.

## D7 — Publication decision

Only after proof, independent checks, significance assessment, and novelty review.

A negative result, collision, or refutation is a valid research outcome at every stage.
