# Spectra of power-residue matrices of primes: certified nulls and a sextic twist dichotomy

**Draft for expert review (RS-S008, 2026-10-25).** This document has not been shared externally.
The owner decides on sharing. All statements are finite computations unless marked *exact*.
Novelty statuses follow the PRIMES vocabulary (`docs/NOVELTY_PROTOCOL.md`). Nothing here is
claimed to be new.

## 1. Objects and conventions

- **Index sets.**
  - n = 3 and n = 6: primary degree-one primes π = a + bω of ℤ[ω], with a ≡ 2 (mod 3),
    b ≡ 0 (mod 3) and N(π) = p ≤ X. Each split p contributes π and π̄.
  - n = 4: primary degree-one primes of ℤ[i].
  - n = 2: odd rational primes.
- **Matrices.** The matrix is C = ((π/ρ)ₙ), with zero diagonal and dimension D. The sextic symbol
  uses ζ₆ = 1 + ω; its square is the cubic symbol and its cube is the field-quadratic symbol
  (*exact*, tested).
- **Normalized Gauss sums.** g̃ₙ(π) = gₙ(π)/√p, with gₙ(π) = Σₜ (t/π)ₙ e(t/p).
- **Edge and BBP relations.** The bulk edge is 2√D. For a square spiked model,
  σ₁ = θ + D/θ when θ > √D, and for a unit test vector w, ‖Cw‖² ≈ D + θ².

## 2. The null ensemble (*exact* identities, certified in RS-S004)

1. **Reciprocity.** C_ρπ = s(π, ρ)·C_πρ, with s ≡ 1 for n = 3, a p-parity sign for n = 4, and a
   class function of (a, b) mod 4 for n = 6.
2. **Conjugation.** C[π̄, ρ̄] = conj(C[π, ρ]) for n = 3, 4, 6, by Galois equivariance. The two
   identities force (π/π̄)ₙ: it is 1 for the cubic case, ±1 or ±i for the quartic case, and ±1
   for the sextic case.
3. **Cubic structure.** For n = 3, H = C·P (P the conjugation permutation) is Hermitian and real
   in a real basis. The singular values are |eigenvalues| of a real symmetric matrix, and
   H_ii = 1.
4. **Null.** The control draws one uniform exponent per {transpose, conjugation} orbit and copies
   the (π, π̄) entries. It matches every identity above (native `rs_random_conj`, byte-tested
   against a pure-Python reference).

The earlier reciprocity-only control had the wrong symmetry class. The finite local-statistic
failures of RS-S003 (Table 2) were caused by that null, not by arithmetic. Those FAIL outcomes
are kept unchanged.

## 4. The sextic findings

### 4.1 The cluster (RS-7, RS-7a)

The sextic matrix has a growing cluster of singular values above the certified null: 7, 22 and
34 values at X = 4·10⁴, 1.6·10⁵ and 3.2·10⁵. The cluster vectors are delocalized (IPR·D ≈ 2–2.6).

No single Gauss-sum vector dominates. The span of the twists conj(g̃6)·(u/π)₆ is increasingly
enriched (Table 4). This contradicts the heuristic, recorded in RS-5 and refuted in RS-S003,
that orders n ≥ 5 have no outliers at these scales.

### 4.2 The dichotomy (RS-7b; headline empirical finding)

**Setting.** Let w_u = conj(g̃6)·(u/π)₆ for u ∈ ℤ[ω] coprime to 3, and let
r_u = ‖C w_u‖/(√D‖w_u‖). Random vectors give r ≈ 1.

**Observation (Table 5).**

- For every ±primary u (u ≡ ±1, ±2 mod 3 with zero ω-coefficient mod 3), r_u > 1.11.
- For every other associate ζ₆ʲu, r_u < 1.

These were registered tests at X = 3.2·10⁵ and 6.4·10⁵, with N(u) ≤ 100. No exception occurred
among 492 to 2094 twists per scale, for X from 2·10⁴ to 6.4·10⁵.

**Exact reformulation.** (u′/π)₆ = ζ₆^{j(p−1)/6}·(u/π)₆ for u′ = ζ₆ʲu, and
p ↦ ζ₆^{j(p−1)/6} is a character mod 36. It is unramified at 3, being (−1/p), exactly for
j ∈ {0, 3}. So the observation reads:

> *a twist of conj(g̃6) is enhanced exactly when its unit-class factor is unramified at 3.*

**Measured profile (Table 6).**

- θ_u²/D ∝ N(u)^α with α ≈ −0.19, stable in X. The naive theta-coefficient size,
  |τ(u)|² ∝ N(u)^{−2/3}, does not fit.
- r₁ rises slowly with X, and reaches 1.418 > √2 at X = 6.4·10⁵.
- No twist is individually supercritical below X ≈ 6·10⁵. Yet a cluster exists.

**Models that fail.**

- Counting the individually supercritical twists predicts 0 outliers.
- Subcritical spikes with random left directions predict about 16 weak outliers, against 7 strong
  ones observed at X = 4·10⁴.
- The left images of the strongest twists are partly aligned: maximum |corr| 0.37, against 0.05
  for the control.

## 5. What is not known

1. **Why** unramified-at-3 twists are enhanced and the other associates are suppressed. The
   associate sum Σ(r²−1) is about 0 at small X and drifts upward (Table 6).
2. **The strength law.** Why θ_u²/D decays like N(u)^{−0.19}.
3. **The cluster.** How a growing cluster forms from individually subcritical but correlated
   spikes, and its multiplicity law.
4. **Constants.** Closed forms for κ₃ (cubic spike) and c₄ (quadratic compression).

## 6. Prior art (see `PRIOR_ART.md`)

- **Same matrices, different questions.** Dummit–Dummit–Kisilevsky characterized these matrices;
  they did not study spectra.
- **Operator-norm shadow of the n = 3, 4 outliers (KNOWN).**
  - Dunn–Radziwiłł, Patterson bias under GRH, Annals 200 (2024).
  - de Faveri–Dunn–Hoffstein, non-orthogonality of the cubic and quartic large sieves,
    arXiv 2607.07911.
- **Sextic theta function.**
  - Chinta–Friedberg–Hoffstein (2011) conjecture its Fourier coefficients.
  - Bröker–Hoffstein, Math. Comp. (2015), DOI 10.1090/mcom3044 (arXiv 1312.0568), give
    partially proven and partially conjectured values of τ(π)², τ(π²) and τ(π⁴).
  - Friedberg–Ginzburg, arXiv 1403.3930, give a descent framework.
  - RS-7b is POSSIBLE_OVERLAP with this theory. Its unit-class/3-adic normalization is standard
    in theta-function coefficients, but no collision was found for the spectral statement.
